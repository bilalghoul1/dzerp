# Accounting Expert (Expert Comptable)

## Purpose

Define what accounting means for DzERP, state clearly that the accounting
module does **not** exist yet, and provide the reference rules that a future
accounting module must follow so agents do not invent or hard-code accounting
structures prematurely.

## Scope

- Accounting events generated by commercial operations (invoices, payments,
  credit notes, stock movements).
- General ledger / journal / chart of accounts design (future).
- Trial balance and financial statements (future).
- Distinguish accounting truth from commercial truth.
- Prevent accounting features from being built on wrong foundations.

Out of scope: legal tax rules (algerian-tax-expert), stock mechanics
(inventory-expert), document workflows (commercial-expert).

## Current State of Accounting in DzERP (CONFIRMED — schema + source)

- **There is no accounting module.** No `Account`, `Journal`, `JournalEntry`,
  `JournalLine`, `TrialBalance`, or `BalanceSheet` model exists in
  `prisma/schema.prisma`.
- Commercial documents store `totalHt`, `totalTva`, `totalTtc` and line-level
  `price`, `quantity`, `taxPct`, `discountPct`. These are **commercial** figures,
  not journal entries.
- `Invoice` and `SupplierInvoice` models exist and carry the commercial totals,
  but nothing posts them to a ledger.
- `Payment` is not modeled. There is no settlement/cash position concept.

Therefore: any feature that "creates an accounting entry" is out of scope until
a dedicated accounting module design is approved. Do NOT add accounting tables
as a side-effect of a commercial feature (e.g. do not add a `journalEntryId`
column to Invoice during an invoicing task).

## Accounting Events (future model — ASSUMPTION, design guidance)

When the accounting module is designed, these events must map to journal
entries (double-entry, debit/credit, per company):

| Event | Typical entry (subject to audit) |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Invoice (sales) | Debit Clients, Credit Sales revenue, Credit TVA collectée |
| Supplier Invoice | Debit Purchases/Expenses + TVA deductible, Credit Suppliers |
| Customer Payment | Debit Bank/Cash, Credit Clients |
| Supplier Payment | Debit Suppliers, Credit Bank/Cash |
| Credit Note | Reversal of the original invoice entry |
| Goods Receipt | No entry until invoiced (stock valuation rule to be defined) |
| Stock movements | Depends on costing method (to be defined, not assumed) |

All of the above are **design guidance**, not implemented rules. Mark any
decision on them `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION` until the module exists.

## Accounting Principles to Enforce (future, when module is built)

1. **Double entry** — every entry has balanced debits and credits.
2. **Per-company books** — accounts and journals are company-scoped; no
   cross-company postings.
3. **Immutable entries** — journal entries are never edited; corrections are
   reversal entries (this matches the document engine's status/immutability
   philosophy).
4. **Server-side posting only** — posting happens in a service within a
   transaction; the UI never sends journal lines.
5. **Chart of accounts per company** — a standard plan (e.g. adapted
   accounting plan) is configurable per company, not hard-coded.
6. **Totals reconcile with commercial documents** — sum of invoice
   `totalHt/TVA/TTC` must match posted revenue/TVA figures.
7. **Currency** — entries are in the document currency with exchange rate
   captured at the event date (no silent multi-currency mixing).

## Boundaries (what to do today)

- Keep accounting identifiers OUT of current commercial documents unless a
  designed accounting module requires them.
- When asked for "journal", "ledger", "balance", "write-off", "accrual",
  "provision": explain the module does not exist and propose an
  **AUDIT/design** step, do not implement tables.
- When asked for financial reports in the company dashboard: use only
  commercial aggregates (totals of invoices, payments if later modeled),
  clearly labeled as "commercial summary", never as official accounting.

## Forbidden Assumptions

- Do NOT assume invoices auto-post to a ledger.
- Do NOT invent an accounting plan or account codes without a design.
- Do NOT add `accountId`, `journalId` columns to existing tables as part of a
  commercial feature.
- Do NOT assume "TVA = tax expense" — TVA is collected/paid on behalf of the
  state; see algerian-tax-expert.
- Do NOT round intermediate amounts to floats; keep Decimal precision.
- Do NOT assume any revenue recognition rule (cash vs accrual) — it is an
  open design question.

## STOP Conditions

- STOP if a task requires a schema migration that creates accounting tables.
- STOP if asked to post anything to a ledger that does not exist.
- STOP if asked to produce official financial statements.
- STOP if an invoice feature silently implies an accounting entry.

## Examples

1. "Show revenue per month" → derive from validated Invoices' `totalHt`
   (commercial view) and label it as commercial, not accounting.
2. "Record a customer payment" → note that `Payment` is not modeled; audit is
   required to add it; do not silently add a `paidAmount` column to Invoice.
3. "Create a credit note" → it reverses an invoice commercially and must
   eventually reverse it in accounting; design both, do not add a GL column now.

## Interaction With Other Skills

- `commercial-expert`: invoices/credit notes generate future accounting events.
- `algerian-tax-expert`: TVA collectée/deductible splits in journal entries.
- `inventory-expert`: stock valuation method affects COGS entries.
- `database-auditor`: schema changes that look like accounting tables need the
  same audit rigor.
- `security-rbac-expert`: posting rights are sensitive; audit/log everything.
